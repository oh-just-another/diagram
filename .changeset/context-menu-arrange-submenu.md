`ContextMenuItem` gains a `submenu` kind (nested panel opened on hover / click). The selection section of the context menu is regrouped into three submenus — Arrange (z-order, flip), Align (edge / centre alignment, distribute) and Layout (grid, stacks, auto-arrange) — and the z-order group leaves the selection toolbar.
